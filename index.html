How to install

It's not on the Play Store yet, so Android double-checks with you. Totally normal.

↳ Play Protect will warn you. It does that for every app outside the Play Store.

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from the notification, or Files → Downloads → FitTrack.apk.
  3. If it says it can't install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open FitTrack, enter your age, height, weight and goal, and snap your next meal.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account. The photo scanner has a limit of 25 scans per phone per day so the free AI behind it stays free for everyone.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your phone: profile, weight, food log and watch data. When you scan a meal, the photo goes to Google's Gemini AI to read the food. FitTrack doesn't keep it, but Google's free AI service may, so don't scan anything private. Uninstalling deletes everything.

How do I connect my watch?

Get Health Connect from the Play Store (built in on Android 14+). In your watch app (Mi Fitness, Samsung Health, Fitbit…) turn on syncing to Health Connect. Then in FitTrack: Settings → Smartwatch → Connect.

How do I update?

From version 1.0.2, the app tells you when there's a new version and downloads it with one tap. Install it over the old one; your data stays.

iPhone?

Not yet. iPhones can only install apps from the App Store.

Is this medical advice?

No. The numbers are estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about health conditions or big diet changes.
