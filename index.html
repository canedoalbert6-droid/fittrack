Install

Ready in two minutes

FitTrack isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks a few extra questions. That's normal.

Google Play Protect warns about every app installed from outside the Play Store. Tap More details → Install anyway.

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from the notification, or go to Files → Downloads → FitTrack.apk.
  3. If your phone blocks unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install.
  5. Open FitTrack, enter your age, height, weight and goal, and snap your next meal.
FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account. Photo scans are limited to 25 per phone per day so the free AI service stays free for everyone.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone: profile, weight, food log and watch data. When you scan a meal, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI to read the food. FitTrack's server doesn't keep it (the app saves a small thumbnail on your phone for your food log), but Google's free AI service may, so don't scan anything private. Uninstalling deletes everything.

How do I connect my smartwatch?

Install Health Connect from the Play Store (built in on Android 14+). In your watch app (Mi Fitness, Samsung Health, Fitbit…) turn on syncing to Health Connect. Then in FitTrack go to Settings → Smartwatch → Connect. The app has step-by-step guides for each brand.

Infinix watches can’t connect yet, because Infinix’s My Health app doesn’t share data with other apps.

How do I update?

From version 1.0.2, the app tells you when a new version is out and downloads it with one tap. Install it over the old one; your data stays.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. iPhones can only install apps from the App Store.

Is this medical advice?

No. The numbers are estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about health conditions or big diet changes.
