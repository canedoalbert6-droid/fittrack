How to install

FitTrack isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks a few extra questions. That's normal.

  1. Open this page on your Android phone in Chrome and tap Download for Android. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Tap the notification, or go to Files → Downloads → FitTrack.apk.
  3. Allow installing. If your phone says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows "Unsafe app blocked" or asks to scan, tap More details → Install anyway. It warns about every app that isn't from the Play Store.
  5. Open FitTrack and fill in your age, height, weight and goal. Done.

Take a photo of your plate and get calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds.

Filipino foods

Adobo, sinigang, silog, pancit and more, with real portions like "1 cup rice".

Smart calorie goal

Learns your real maintenance calories from what you eat and how your weight moves.

Smartwatch sync

Reads steps, active calories, heart rate and sleep through Health Connect.

Budget meal ideas

Short on protein? Cheap recipes from what's in your kitchen, or what to grab at 7-Eleven.

Reminders

Morning weigh-in, meal reminders and an evening protein nudge. All optional.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The food scanner allows 25 photo scans per phone per day so the free AI service stays free for everyone.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only: your profile, weight, food log and watch data never leave it. When you scan a meal, that photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI to read the food. FitTrack doesn't keep it, but Google's free AI service may store it, so don't scan anything private. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

How do I connect my smartwatch?

Install Health Connect from the Play Store (built in on Android 14+). In your watch app (Mi Fitness, Samsung Health, Fitbit…) turn on syncing to Health Connect. Then in FitTrack go to Settings → Smartwatch → Connect and allow the permissions.

How do I update?

Download the new version from this page and install it over the old one. Your data stays.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. iPhones can only install apps from the App Store.

Is this medical advice?

No. Calorie and nutrient numbers are estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about health conditions or big diet changes.
